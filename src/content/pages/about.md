I'm Satyajit, a systems engineer in the San Francisco Bay Area. I spent the first part of my career in graphics drivers and shader compilers (AMD, then Intel). These days I write about the infrastructure that serves large language models.

This site collects deep dives on:

- inference engines and their schedulers
- tool-call parsing and streaming
- LoRA serving
- constrained decoding

Older posts on compilers, virtual machines and a few self-driving-car and Kaggle projects are still here, under [archives](/archives) and [tags](/tags).
